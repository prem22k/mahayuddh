# Surface brief: public landing (/welcome)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a developer arriving cold (often from a shared Pass) who preps for interviews and grinds alone. Job: understand in seconds that Mahayuddh is daily DSA practice with their own people, then start a squad. Primary action: "Start a squad" (sign-up, next=/squad); secondary "Join with a code". Constraints: no invented claims or user counts; example data is labeled Example; product voice and banned words from DESIGN.md apply. User confirmed 2026-11-03: keep the app's look, but the landing may use light, depth, scroll-driven camera moves and big motion that the app itself avoids. User rejected structures that read as effects or "AI slop" (3D grids, film credits, tearing tickets) and asked not to copy or drift toward Zync.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a run of short, very large sentences, each followed by a real, working piece of Mahayuddh the visitor can use right there. It refuses the category default of hero + screenshot + grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Mahayuddh v4 at stage scale: obsidian canvas, hairline surfaces, perforated Passes, Space Grotesk set up to 6rem with tight tracking, JetBrains Mono numerals inside sentences, gold only for the single go action and the live timer, emerald only for verified, indigo for patterns and Sarathi's nudges. Each piece sits in its own pool of warm overhead light on the dark stage.

STORY: Practice DSA with your people → try today's battle (a real timer) → Priya solved it in 14:22 (proof, the stamp) → see how she thought (reveal code) → stuck? Sarathi asks, never answers → Rahul thinks you can't beat 38:11 (challenge) → fourteen days, nobody has missed one (squad streak) → no XP, no coins, no levels → bring your people.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: the headline runs two lines across ~11 of 12 columns at 6rem, top-left. Under it sits a short subline plus the gold "Start a squad" and ghost "Join with a code". The Battle Pass for No. 518 Coin Change II begins in the lower third, right-offset, lit from above, with a working "Try it" timer. Mobile: a 3rem headline, the CTAs, then a portrait Pass whose top half is visible.

FORM: "Big statements, real pieces", surface-scope candidate 6 of 7 (THE ROLL after one re-roll), seed key 0a5e3937. Signature interaction: pressing "Try it" starts a real battle timer that follows the visitor down the page as the product's solve dock (mobile: capsule). Submitting it feeds their own time into the Proof card and today's streak dot. Motion grammar: statements light up word by word as they cross the reading line, and pieces rise and un-tilt into their light. The stamp is the one authored moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
